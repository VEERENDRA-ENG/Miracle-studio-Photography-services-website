Common Questions

Package & Booking FAQs

How does Miracle Studio handle pricing?

Every event is unique in scope, location, and coverage hours. To avoid generic or inaccurate pricing, we provide transparent, itemized quotes tailored directly to your shoot requirements.

How do we receive our final photographs?

You will receive access to a private, password-protected online gallery with high-resolution download options. For wedding and heirloom packages, physical albums are handcrafted and hand-delivered.

Can packages be customized?

Yes! Any package can be customized with additional hours, extra photographers, drone captures, or bespoke album specifications.

Ready to Discuss Your Package?

Tell us about your date and requirements, and our studio coordinator will prepare an estimate.
